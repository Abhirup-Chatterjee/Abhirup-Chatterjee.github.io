M sends (u : v : 1) on π₁ to (x′ : y′ : 1) on π₂ = {z = 0}. Its bottom row cuts out the vanishing line on π₁ (points level with L), which goes to the line at infinity. Points on π₁ ∩ π₂ (green) are fixed. Set tilt = 0 to recover the affine case.
