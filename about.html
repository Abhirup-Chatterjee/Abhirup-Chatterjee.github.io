About

Hello, I am Abhirup.

I am a PhD student at The Institute of Mathematical Sciences, Chennai, working with Prof. Anirban Mukhopadhyay. My research sits in analytic number theory over function fields: how arithmetic functions such as the Möbius function are distributed when the integers are replaced by polynomials over a finite field.

Before the PhD I did my M.Sc. at IMSc, with a thesis on the Marcus–Spielman–Srivastava solution to the Kadison–Singer problem, and my B.Sc. at Ramakrishna Mission Residential College, Narendrapur. The M.Sc. thesis was my first taste of how much a problem about operators can turn on the behaviour of polynomials; the function-field side of number theory, where the Riemann hypothesis is a theorem and polynomials are the integers, is where I have stayed. Away from the desk I fold paper and make things, and this site is where the two meet.

Research interests

Current

Number theory over function fields

The ring $\mathbb{F}_q[t]$ behaves remarkably like $\mathbb{Z}$, but with the Riemann hypothesis available as a theorem. I use that extra structure to study problems that remain out of reach over the integers.

Current

Distribution of arithmetic functions

Correlations and mean values of multiplicative functions, especially the Möbius function and its truncations, in the spirit of the Chowla conjecture.

Also interested in

L-functions and modular forms

Analytic properties and mean values of L-functions, and the arithmetic of modular forms.

Also interested in

$p$-adic analysis

Analysis over non-archimedean fields and its interplay with arithmetic.

Papers & preprints

Preprint · 2026

Chowla Conjecture for the truncated Möbius function over $\mathbb{F}_q[t]$

Abhirup Chatterjee

We study the two-point correlation of the truncated Möbius function $\mu_M$ over $\mathbb{F}_q[t]$ by transforming the problem into a uniform bivariate Erdős–Kac theorem in terms of characteristic functions. See also the Erdős–Kac Lab.
